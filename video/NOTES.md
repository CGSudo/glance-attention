# Glance Intro - Continuous Camera & Morph Transitions

1. Beat 1 to 2 (Studio to Squint): Soft camera push-in on easel; painter hand rises from bottom foreground, fingers stretching and rotating to frame the composition without cuts.
2. Beat 2 to 3 (Squint to Light Bloom): Hand pinches down to squint aperture; world progressively desaturates and blurs behind the aperture; brightest window light expands into an overblown bloom polygon.
3. Beat 3 to 4 (Bloom to Warning): Blooming polygon coordinates morph directly into an equilateral warning triangle; background color snaps to hazard yellow while halftone and scanline tears burst across the frame.
4. Beat 4 to 5 (Warning to Attention Heatmap): Triangle vertices expand and unfold back into the original 1000x650 canvas bounds; full color restores as teal heatmap contours and gaze sequence dots 1->4 bloom progressively from face to note.
5. Beat 5 to 6 (Heatmap to Glance App): Camera pulls back smoothly as the easel recedes; canvas embeds into the Glance app window on a laptop mockup; heatmap toggles with brand lockup and URL.
