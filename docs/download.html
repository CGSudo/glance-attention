Your first analysis

  1. Open Models and download a supported model. A connection is required for this step.
  2. Drop a screenshot into Glance; inspect the selected model and heatmap.
  3. Draw an area around your main message, then review its attention share.

After models are installed, analysis is designed to work offline. Runtime behavior and model export availability still need verification.
