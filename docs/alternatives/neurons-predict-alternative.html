When Neurons Predict is the better choice

Choose Neurons Predict if you need a vendor-supported creative testing process and a customer success contact. Neurons advertises customer success support, with brand kit and SSO depending on package or add-on. Verify benchmark reports and procurement terms in a demo.

Bring one campaign asset to the demo. Ask how brand elements are identified, how results are shared and which claims describe observed behavior versus estimates. Glance does not replace a managed research relationship or enterprise procurement process.

Ask the vendor to demonstrate your required workflow before cancelling a plan. Glance’s published model references help you inspect the method; they do not establish that two products produce the same map.

Read accuracy claims with the denominator

For a ceiling-normalized benchmark headline, their headline % is a ratio to the human-agreement ceiling on a public benchmark. In plain terms: the model score is divided by the score describing how well people agree with one another. That differs from counting correct predictions on your page.

This explains a reporting method, not a verified formula for every vendor claim. Ask Neurons Predict for the metric, dataset and denominator. Glance shows its sources and raw benchmark references beside the human ceiling; reference model results are not measured Glance results. Switch in 2 minutes

Start with one image. Two minutes is a walkthrough goal; downloads and setup can take longer.

  1. Export an image you have permission to analyze. Keep existing reports.
  2. Open Glance in your browser for a first check, or check the desktop download for offline use.
  3. Inspect your headline and main action. Record the model and areas of interest before changing one element.
  4. Compare the revised image. Use the result as a hypothesis for a user test, not a promise of higher conversion.

Frequently asked questions

Is Glance a free Neurons Predict alternative?

Yes, for local attention prediction without a subscription. Glance is open source and in development; check the browser demo and download status before replacing a production workflow.

Can I keep client images offline?

Desktop inference runs locally after model download. First-time model/runtime downloads need a connection. External integrations have their own privacy boundaries.

Do I need an account?

No Glance account is required. The linked vendor service has its own access and commercial terms.

Is Glance more accurate than Neurons Predict?

No direct performance comparison is established. They belong to the same broad family of research-based saliency methods; this does not establish identical models or results. Compare raw metrics, dataset and human agreement ceiling.

Can I import existing projects and scores?

No automatic competitor project migration is promised. Export your source images, re-run analysis and retain the model name. Scores from different tools are not interchangeable.
